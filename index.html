§ 02 — Selected work

From schematics to syllabi — what I've shipped lately.

/ robotics · education

Wall-Dodger Bot

Arduino obstacle-avoiding rover for a teen STEM workshop. 3D-printed 200mm chassis, HC-SR04 ultrasonic, L293D motor driver. Lesson plan included.

/ web · estate

Islamic Will Generation

Meeras — draft an Islamic will in the browser with guided steps. Free, open source, and your data stays on this device until you export.
/ web · fiqh

Islamic Inheritance Calculator

Faraid distribution with madhhab notes and citations at /mcalc. Built to be auditable — every share shows the rule behind it.
/ pwa · hajj

Manasik — Hajj Companion

Offline-first guide for pilgrims: 13 rites in English and Arabic, Miqāt boundaries, preparation checklists, and tap-to-call emergency numbers. Installs to your phone.
/ productivity · health

Life Planner for ADHD

A planner built around ADHD brains — short loops, dopamine-aware structure, no shame on missed days. Currently in prototype.

/ hardware · maker

Workshop Build Kits

Curated kits for STEM sessions: parts list, 3D-printable files, Tinkercad simulations, ready to run with teens (10–16).
